# Review: promising experiment, unfinished world

Review date: 15 November 2026. Gameplay source: `d3b83c54495c5dff3ffe9b8cab33f4e7df0cfff0` in draft PR #7. This verdict is an editorial comparison to the user-selected direction, not a claim of complete independent playtesting.

## Verdict

The prototype represents the **research-puzzle foundation** of the vision. It represents only a small part of the **art, exploration, character and emotional ambition**. Keep the causal loop and useful engineering. Do not use the current screenshot as the visual acceptance standard.

| Dimension | Evidence and judgment | Next useful move |
|---|---|---|
| Research becomes gameplay | Distinct score/delivery state, physical couriers, interventions and reversible trials support a real experiment. This fits the brief. | Preserve causality while making observation more environmental and less interface-led. |
| Art direction | The palette gestures toward gold, ivory, teal and oxblood. Flat mountain polygons and repeated house forms lack the depth, material and specificity of the references. | Establish one layered scene with an unmistakable architectural landmark and carefully directed light. |
| World exploration | The route joins stations and a ferry; the broader ecology and branching discovery remain largely proposed. | Add a useful vantage point or side discovery before expanding to another region. |
| Pip | Bounded observations are valuable. The simple rendering keeps some color and ear cues but loses much of the reference's organic silhouette and presence. | Refine the rig and give noticing, following, uncertainty and reunion distinct readable actions. |
| Inhabited place | The kitchen changes, residents gather, and an extra bowl provides a promising emotional consequence. Repeated resident figures weaken it. | Give the affected resident a distinctive appearance and a staged response to the first real delivery. |
| Danger and wonder | The current harbor reads as a friendly schematic town. The larger latent-world mystery is not yet strongly felt. | Introduce a visible distant anomaly or uncertain passage with a readable local clue; avoid gratuitous combat or oppressive darkness. |

## What was inspected

AUXILI inspected the live opening and field-letter interaction, the PR's changed-file list and exact head, the renderer source, the saved opening screenshot, the four-plate art direction and character references, and Spine's handoff/test receipts. This pass did not complete a new full playthrough or independently verify all recorded performance figures or audio.

Spine's recorded self-review reports build/type checks, eight tests and an input-driven Chromium playthrough covering a failed trial, recovery, six deliveries, saved progress and the ferry ending. It explicitly leaves full audiovisual review and independent newcomer comprehension unfinished. Those are author-reported results, not independent acceptance.

## Recommended next acceptance target

A short, beautiful, genuinely animated arrival-to-consequence sequence. The next agent should be able to retain its stronger existing game systems while importing the encounter's causal properties and this visual direction. The first moment must make someone want to step into the world; the first experiment must reward curiosity; the consequence must make the discovery matter to someone.

The added direction package fixes a handoff gap. **It does not, by itself, fix the game's artwork.**
