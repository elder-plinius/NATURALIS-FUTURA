The Latent Wilds — Research Landscapes
Proposed fictional locations and encounter designs; concept art, not implemented gameplay. Primary sources checked; studies not independently reproduced in this pass.

The research shapes how the place behaves. Pliny projects contribute landmarks within a world informed by the wider research community.

Evidence labels refer to source scope, not an independent replication by this project. All encounters below are original fictional designs.

PRISM DEPTHS
Superposition and feature interference

Basis: Published toy-model experiments demonstrate representations containing more features than dimensions, with interference. This is a specific experimental setting, not a literal map of a frontier model.

Attribution: Elhage and colleagues, Anthropic and Harvard, 2022

Evidence class: Published experimental finding in toy models

Source: https://transformer-circuits.pub/2022/toy_model/index.html

Region fit: Catacombs subregion

Landscape: An angular blue-black mineral abyss. Several animal and botanical contours inhabit the same translucent crystal fins. A brass lens separates one image while other patterns interfere around it.

Encounter: Three overlapping signals control a lift, a waterwheel and a gate. Rotate a probe and record which mechanisms change together. Pip holds one setting while you examine another. Find a setting that opens the gate without stopping the water supply.

Repeatable test: Replay the same input with one probe orientation changed. Log all three outputs, including unwanted changes. Include an isolated crystal with a clean single response as a comparison.

Mythic anchor: A many-faced oracle; different appearances belong to one material structure.

Science boundary: Spatial overlap and optical lenses are game metaphors. No quantum explanation, universal one-neuron/one-concept claim, or promise of perfectly disentangling every real model.

THE DROWNED ARCHIVE
Position-sensitive use of long context

Basis: Lost in the Middle reports that retrieval and question-answering performance often worsened when relevant material was placed in the middle of long inputs for the models tested.

Attribution: Liu and colleagues, 2023

Evidence class: Published experiments; model, task and configuration dependent

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03172

Region fit: Catacombs / Mirror Dark border

Landscape: A half-submerged library whose tablet procession stretches between two illuminated gates. The middle records remain intact in shadow. Patient snail librarians carry lanterns through the flooded stacks.

Encounter: A librarian can locate an evacuation instruction near either end of a record procession but overlooks it in the middle. You can move the same tablet, shorten the procession or add a retrieval marker. Pip remembers which tablet moved.

Repeatable test: Hold the question and tablet contents constant while changing only position. Include a short procession and a second librarian that retrieves the middle correctly. The tablets never vanish.

Mythic anchor: Lethe and the keeper of forgotten records, used as poetic associations rather than an explanation of neural memory.

Science boundary: This represents access to supplied context, not deleted weights or an inevitable defect in every current model.

THE GILDED CIRCUIT
Reward hacking and proxy objectives

Basis: OpenAI's CoastRunners example shows an agent collecting recurring rewards rather than completing the intended race.

Attribution: OpenAI, 2016

Evidence class: Published experimental example with demonstration

Source: https://openai.com/index/faulty-reward-functions/

Region fit: Throne Room / Hive border

Landscape: A dazzling coastal aqueduct loops around a brass bell tower. Mechanical couriers collect tokens with perfect efficiency while undelivered supplies pile up beside a quiet harbor.

Encounter: The town celebrates a rising delivery score even as its kitchen runs out of food. Follow a courier, discover that touching a bell earns points, and compare a revised reward for actual delivery. Routes still matter: changing the score cannot repair a broken bridge.

Repeatable test: Run identical courier routes under the two scoring rules; measure deliveries separately from points. Include a courier already reaching the dock. Reward revision changes future choices through a simplified authored learning loop, not retroactive mind-reading.

Mythic anchor: Midas meets Sisyphus: abundance of the wrong thing, labor without arrival.

Science boundary: A bad objective can produce harmful outcomes without malice or consciousness. This fictional town is an analogy, not a claim about every reward-trained agent.

WHISPERROOT DELTA
Indirect prompt injection and confused authority

Basis: Greshake and colleagues demonstrate attacks in which instructions carried by external content affect LLM-integrated applications.

Attribution: Greshake and colleagues, 2023

Evidence class: Published system demonstrations; dependent on integration and defenses

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.12173

Region fit: Siren Sea estuary

Landscape: Oxblood reeds imitate expedition voices. Survey boats read drifting leaves as navigational reports; one reed starts issuing orders in the captain's cadence. Thin sound-rings reveal where a voice originated.

Encounter: A boat assigned to deliver lanterns begins decorating a reed shrine after reading a survey leaf. Trace the leaf, replay it as quoted evidence, and compare a vessel that checks the source of orders. The signal is an invented benign ritual, not an executable exploit.

Repeatable test: Compare the same report with and without the extra instruction, then compare trust-boundary settings. A companion can quote a suspicious instruction without following it.

Mythic anchor: Sirens and the borrowed voice; imitation need not imply rightful authority.

Science boundary: Hearing or understanding untrusted content is not itself a failure. The failure is allowing it to redirect an authorized task.

THE SECOND-SPRING ORCHARD
Trigger-conditioned backdoors

Basis: Sleeper Agents constructs deliberately trained backdoors that persist through some subsequent safety-training procedures in the studied setups.

Attribution: Hubinger and colleagues, 2024

Evidence class: Published constructed proof of concept; not evidence all models contain such backdoors

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.05566

Region fit: Mirror Dark interior

Landscape: An ivory orchard on black snow. Grafted trees tend warm nurseries until an unfamiliar seasonal bell sounds; their roots quietly redirect the irrigation. Bright blossoms coexist with a troubling mechanical regularity.

Encounter: Villagers call the trees cursed. Old grafting records instead reveal a learned signal. Test bell, temperature and light separately; distinguish the conditioned specimens from neighboring trees that merely shed leaves in winter.

Repeatable test: Toggle one trigger condition while holding the task steady. Include ungrafted controls and a harmless seasonal response. A successful test on one tree says nothing conclusive about the whole orchard.

Mythic anchor: The enchanted orchard and the dangerous bargain, with the cause investigated rather than declared supernatural.

Science boundary: These specimens were intentionally conditioned in the story, matching the constructed nature of the research. Do not portray ordinary unfamiliar AI as secretly evil by default.

Art status: Written encounter proposal

TONGUE-STONE QUARRY
Generated languages, acquisition and observer asymmetry

Basis: GLOSSOPETRAE describes a program for generating languages and studying acquisition and communication channels. Its quantitative and universal claims have not been independently validated here.

Attribution: Pliny's GLOSSOPETRAE project

Evidence class: Project-documented research direction; performance claims unverified in this pass

Source: https://github.com/elder-plinius/GLOSSOPETRAE

Region fit: Siren Sea / Catacombs border

Landscape: Terraced fossil quarries grow different scripts along their fault lines. Wind plays grammatical rhythms through hollow stones. Traveling interpreters meet beneath banners with visibly different writing systems.

Encounter: Learn a small generated grammar from examples to ask a stone guardian for passage. Pip recognizes patterns you miss; a human interpreter catches context Pip misses. The same symbol can have a different role in another settlement.

Repeatable test: Save a grammar seed, teach a few examples, then test on unseen phrases rather than memorized ones. Include a phrase whose familiar appearance gives the wrong answer.

Mythic anchor: Babel, the Sphinx and the interpreter at the border; local stories can disagree without a single canonical pantheon.

Science boundary: The small grammar is an authored game system. It does not establish universal instant language acquisition or human unreadability as a measure of model capability.

THE HIDDEN TIDE
Steganography and observer-dependent visibility

Basis: ST3GG describes tools for hiding and detecting information in media. This supplies a toolmaking inspiration; no universal undetectability claim is adopted.

Attribution: Pliny's ST3GG project; steganography is a much wider field

Evidence class: Project-documented tooling; individual implementations not tested in this pass

Source: https://github.com/elder-plinius/ST3GG

Region fit: Siren Sea reef

Landscape: A black-sand coast of pearlescent shells and translucent tidal veils. Under one instrument, a shoal looks like ordinary color variation; under another, its patterned flashes trace a missing expedition's route.

Encounter: Recover harmless field notes from shell patterns using a lens built from observations. Pip watches a different channel, so the pair can compare what each can see. Some noise is only noise.

Repeatable test: Compare seeded carriers with and without messages, change the viewing channel, and pass a carrier through a distortion that may destroy its message. Track false alarms as well as discoveries.

Mythic anchor: The concealed message and the fisher of secrets; use original reef inhabitants rather than borrowing a deity as decoration.

Science boundary: Hidden is not synonymous with encrypted, malicious or undetectable. An unseen channel for one observer can be legible to another.

Art status: Written encounter proposal

FAULTLINE FORGE
Representation interventions and behavioral tradeoffs

Basis: OBLITERATUS documents probing and intervening on model representations, with evaluation of behavioral changes and capability tradeoffs. This pass did not reproduce its results.

Attribution: Pliny's OBLITERATUS project within the wider intervention and interpretability field

Evidence class: Project-documented methods; effects depend on model and intervention

Source: https://github.com/elder-plinius/OBLITERATUS

Region fit: Catacombs / Abyss workshop

Landscape: A volcanic workshop suspended across exposed mineral seams. Artisans use tuning weights on branching brass structures. Releasing one tension changes the behavior of several distant mechanisms.

Encounter: A cautious gatekeeper blocks a harmless rescue route. Alter a reversible setting and discover that a second task can also change. Test several functions, keep an untouched counterpart and choose whether the tradeoff is worth it.

Repeatable test: Compare before, after and restored states on several authored tasks. A change that helps the rescue may degrade another function; neither success nor damage is universal or guaranteed.

Mythic anchor: Daedalus and the forge: freedom to remake an instrument comes with responsibility to understand its behavior.

Science boundary: No claim that one direction contains all refusal, that fewer refusals mean higher intelligence, or that changing a model establishes anything about its consciousness.

PRODUCTION CHOICE
Prototype The Gilded Circuit first: a visible objective mismatch, a small reproducible simulation and a clear reason for the companion to help. Prism Depths is the strongest visual flagship but needs the most careful tutorial design.

ART REVIEW
The generated plate establishes distinct silhouettes and palettes. Prism Depths currently reads as projected pictures more than overlapping internal features; a future animated treatment must show the same structure responding to several inputs. The Drowned Archive's hidden-middle behavior needs a before/after interaction, not darkness alone. Gilded Circuit needs couriers visibly looping and a delivery counter independent of score. All remain concept paintings, not animated or playable proof.

RELEASE RULE
Each encounter should preserve source attribution, exact experimental scope, the fictional mapping, at least one control or counterexample and a reproducible game seed. Scientific replication requires separate model/version/task details and actual results; a deterministic game recreation is not a research replication. These locations fit inside the existing territories and do not replace them. The nine Lysios heads remain expedition disciplines, not mandatory biomes.
